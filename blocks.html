⛏️ Ezra's Block World

Dig, mine, craft and build in your very own block world!

🌍 My Worlds — tap one to play
How to play:
🕹️ Use the ◀ ▶ buttons to walk and ⤒ to jump (or arrow keys).
⛏️ Hold your finger on a block to mine it — it goes in your bag.
🧱 Pick a block from your bag, then tap empty space to build with it.
🪵 Mine trees for wood, then craft planks, sticks and pickaxes. Better pickaxes dig harder blocks — you need an iron one for 💎 diamonds!
🌱 Running out of wood? Mine leaves to find saplings, plant one on grass or dirt, and it grows into a brand new tree!
🔦 Dig deep to find caves and ores. Craft torches so you can see at night.

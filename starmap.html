🔭 Antarctic Star-Map

The sky over the South Pole is a jigsaw of the Earth — and the pieces blew away.

✨ 0 🌍 0/0 📮 0
You are the astronomer at an Antarctic research station.
🧊 Hunt the ice for meteorites — Antarctica really is the best place on Earth to find them, because dark rocks show up on white ice.
🚀 Each one holds a lost constellation. Fly up and gather its stars.
🔍 Work out which country it belongs to from the clues.
🌍 Then spin it the right way up and place it on the star-map to put the world back together.
Every constellation is made from that country's real cities.

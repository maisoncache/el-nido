🛡️ Safe & Private

What this site does and doesn't do. In plain English.

The short version: this site asks you for nothing, knows nothing about you, and can't send anything anywhere. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no adverts, no chat, and no way for anyone to message anyone else.

What we collect about you

Nothing. No name, no email, no age, no location, no account. You never type anything into this site except inside the games themselves, and that stays on your own device.

How the play counts and reactions work

You can tap ❤️ Loved it, 🤯 Tricky! or 🔁 Played it again on any game. Those are the only choices — there is no comment box and no star rating, on purpose. That means nothing unkind can ever be posted here, and there is nothing to moderate.

What gets counted is just a number going up. It isn't tied to you in any way, because there's nothing to tie it to.

Where your saved games live

Every game saves your progress in your own browser, on your own device, using something called local storage. It never leaves your device, and we can't read it. Clearing your browser data clears it. Using a different device means starting fresh.

What this site can't do

  • No adverts, ever.
  • No trackers, analytics companies or cookies from anyone else.
  • No chat, messages, comments or usernames.
  • No links out to other websites, videos or social media.
  • No uploads, downloads or file sharing between people.

This isn't just a promise. Every page carries a rule (a Content-Security-Policy) that the browser itself enforces: pages here are only allowed to load things from this site. Even if somebody tried to add an advert or a tracker, the browser would block it.

The video

The movie is hosted on this site and plays in a plain video player. It isn't on YouTube or anywhere else, so there are no adverts before it, no "up next" suggestions afterwards, and nobody is watching who watched.

Who made it

Everything here — all the games, all the artwork, the language, the movie — was designed by one kid, and built with help. It's made for playing, not for profit. There is nothing to buy and nothing to sign up to.
